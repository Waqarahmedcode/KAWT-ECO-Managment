Camp Notes
548 patients served in one day — fifth camp of the programme, first at Thatta, Sindh (Goath Chatto Chand, Pang/Panjh Mori). OFFICIAL FIGURES: Total camp cost PKR 419,506 = Operational PKR 151,700 (36.2%, Non-Zakat Fund) + Medicines PKR 267,806 (63.8%, Zakat Fund). Cost per patient PKR 766. Zero emergency cases logged. Beneficiary mix: 42% children under 14, 11% elderly 60+ — combined vulnerable groups were 53% of all patients. Camp Operating Model: community arrival with category count at entry, doctor consultation and clinical screening, basic tests where available, free Zakat-funded medicines after consultation, and referral/follow-up guidance for unresolved cases. Report reporting note: the four demographic categories (adult male, adult female, children, elderly) sum to 548 and were logged as patient categories at entry — a separate sex-wise register was not confirmed to prove children/elderly were also split by gender. Location risk note: Thatta district carries a high river-flood hazard classification (ThinkHazard/World Bank GFDRR) — future repeat camps at this site should pre-check access routes, weather/flood alerts, drinking-water availability and ambulance reach before deployment. Next-camp recommendations from the official report: track diagnosis categories, medicine stock usage (opening/issued/closing/wastage) and referral outcomes, not only patient counts.
